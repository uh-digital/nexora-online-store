Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Nexora handles information when you use our website and services.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nexora. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our online store. This Privacy Policy describes the general information practices used by Nexora throughout this website.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Nexora, the website may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address used for account registration.
  • Account login information.
  • Products added to your cart or wishlist.
  • Information submitted through contact forms.
  • Basic website usage information.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and improve the Nexora shopping experience, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing shopping and wishlist functionality.
  • Responding to customer inquiries.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.
  • Communicating important service information.

4. Account Information

If you create an account on Nexora, certain account information may be stored by the website to support registration, login and account-related features. Users should keep their account credentials secure and should not share their passwords with others.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Nexora may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage to support website functionality. These technologies can be used to remember preferences, account states, cart items and wishlist items during your browsing experience.

6. Data Security

We aim to maintain appropriate safeguards for information handled through the website. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

Nexora may use third-party technologies or services to support website functionality. Such services may have their own privacy policies and terms, which users should review when applicable.

8. Children's Privacy

Nexora is designed as a general gaming and creator gear store and does not intentionally restrict ordinary browsing based solely on age. Parents or guardians are encouraged to supervise minors when using online shopping services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website develops. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Nexora support team through our Contact page.
